Security & Compliance

HIPAA Compliant by Design,
Not by Afterthought

Security isn't a feature we added — it's the foundation we built on. Every layer of ClawHealth is engineered for healthcare-grade data protection.

Per-Patient Encryption

Every patient gets an isolated, encrypted container. Data at rest uses AES-256 via SQLCipher. Data in transit is protected with TLS 1.3. Even we can't read your health data.

HIPAA BAA Coverage

Full Business Associate Agreements with all infrastructure providers including AI model providers. Complete audit trails for every data access, meeting the strictest compliance requirements.

Complete Audit Trails

Every interaction, data access, and AI decision is logged immutably. HIPAA-required audit trails are maintained automatically with tamper-evident checksums.

Patient Data Isolation

Each patient runs in a dedicated Docker container with memory limits, network isolation, and zero shared state. A breach of one patient cannot affect another.

Safety Validation Layer

Every AI response passes through a secondary clinical safety validator before reaching the patient. Emergency keywords trigger immediate escalation. When in doubt, we escalate to a human.

FHIR R4 Compliance

All health data follows HL7 FHIR R4 standards for maximum interoperability. ICD-10 coded conditions, structured medication records, and standardized vital formats.
